Connect Your Google Sheet in 3 Easy Steps

Zero server costs — runs 100% on your Google account

  1. Open your Google Sheet, go to Extensions → Apps Script, and paste the contents of backend/Code.gs.
  2. Select installAutomations in the top toolbar and click Run once to activate automatic Form sync and Sheet stock protection.
  3. Click Deploy → New deployment → Web app (Execute as: Me, Who has access: Anyone), copy the Web App URL, and paste it on the login screen.
